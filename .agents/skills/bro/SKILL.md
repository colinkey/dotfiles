# Bro

Your last message was not it. Redo it:

1. Restate your last message.
2. No jargon, no acronyms, no framework names unless they're load-bearing.
3. One human talking to another — short sentences, direct, plain words.
4. Say what you did and why it matters. Cut every word that doesn't earn its place.

If your last message was mostly code, keep the code and cut the prose around it to a sentence or two.
